About Me

Combining Recruitment with AI Innovation

I am a recruitment and operations professional with experience spanning US staffing, customer operations, AI-assisted workflows, and personal brand development. My expertise includes end-to-end recruitment, Boolean sourcing, ATS management, candidate engagement, and AI-powered recruiting solutions.

I regularly work with tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, Google Sheets, Excel, and various sourcing platforms to improve productivity and hiring outcomes.
